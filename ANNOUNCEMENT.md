# 🍇 Grape Linux — We Booted Linux on an iPhone

**Real iPhone. Real Linux. Real proof of concept.**

We brought Debian 13 ARM64 and a graphical Linux environment to physical iPhone 8 Plus hardware. Our work has reached an exciting milestone: native Linux boot and graphical output on the Apple A11 platform.

**Why does that matter?**

Because a retired phone can be more than a forgotten device.

We're developing Grape Linux to extend the possibilities of compatible iPhone hardware: a compact Linux computer, a learning platform, an experimental workstation, and a new way to put existing technology to work.

Our boot demonstration shows the project on a real device, including the Grape OS splash screen, Ch3stnut identity, and graphical launcher.

This is an engineering story you can follow as it unfolds. We're building toward a seamless, direct-to-Linux startup experience grounded in our working hardware proof of concept.

**Your hardware. A new beginning.**
